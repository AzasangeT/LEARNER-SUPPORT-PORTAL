About Us

Learn more about Skills Track and our mission to provide learners with a simple and accessible platform for managing their learning journey.

WHO WE ARE

Supporting Learners Every Step of the Way

Skills Track is a Learner Support Portal designed to help learners manage their academic responsibilities and access support in one central platform.

The portal brings important learning activities together, including tasks, support requests, announcements, resources and progress tracking.

Our goal is to make it easier for learners to stay organised, keep track of their responsibilities and communicate with their assessors when they need assistance.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide learners with a central platform where they can manage their learning activities, access useful resources and receive academic support.


Our Vision

We aim to create a learning environment where learners can stay organised, monitor their progress and get the support they need to succeed.

OUR PURPOSE

Why We Built Skills Track

We wanted to bring the most important parts of the learner experience together in one place.
