Project Team Feedback / Review

Overall, I feel that our team made good progress during the first month of the project. We were able to work together to understand the problem we were trying to solve and develop an idea for a Student Support Portal that could make it easier for learners and assessors to manage tasks, progress, support requests, and documents.

One of the things that went well was that we were able to share ideas and contribute towards the planning and design of the project. We discussed the different features that the system should have and started working on the structure and user interface of the portal. Using tools such as Figma and GitHub also helped us understand how to plan and collaborate on a software development project.

We did experience some challenges, especially with communication, coordinating our different tasks, and making sure that everyone understood their responsibilities. At times, some tasks took longer than expected, and we had to spend additional time explaining certain technical concepts and resolving GitHub or coding issues. However, these challenges also helped us learn how important communication, teamwork, and proper planning are when working on a group project.

I think our team can improve by communicating more regularly, clearly dividing responsibilities, and setting deadlines for individual tasks. We should also make sure that everyone understands the work they contribute rather than depending on one team member to complete or explain it.

Overall, I believe the project has been a valuable learning experience. We have gained a better understanding of JavaScript, Firebase, GitHub, project planning, UI design, and the software development life cycle. I am looking forward to seeing how we can improve our teamwork and develop the project further during the next stage.
