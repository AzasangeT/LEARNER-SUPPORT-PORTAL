Research

Research Topic: Learner Support Portal
Purpose of Research

Research was conducted to understand the needs of learners and assessors and to identify suitable technologies for developing the portal.

Key Findings
Learners need a central place to manage learning activities.
Assessors need an easier way to monitor learner progress.
Support requests should have clear statuses so learners know what is happening.
Authentication is required to protect learner information.
Firebase can be used for authentication and storing application data.
A dashboard can provide learners with a quick overview of their activities and progress.
Conclusion

The research supports the development of a centralised Learner Support Portal that combines learner activities, progress tracking and support requests into one system.
